## `/partners/` — White-label bookkeeping for CPA firms

**Experiment:** EXP-005 (CONTENT)  
**Primary Intent:** white label bookkeeping for CPA firms  
**Current Depth:** 374 prose words  
**Checkpoint:** 12 weeks after deploy  
**Priority:** 1 of 5 (highest contract value)

### Commercial Terms (must have before drafting)

- [ ] **Pricing model** — per hour, per client, per FTE, or retainer? Actual ranges, not "competitive pricing."
- [ ] **Minimum engagement** — floor in hours or clients, if any
- [ ] **Contract terms** — notice period, ramp-up and ramp-down terms

### Delivery Capabilities (must have before drafting)

- [ ] **Onboarding timeline** — is the CPA-firm path same as direct clients (2–3 weeks)?
- [ ] **Staffing model** — dedicated staff per firm, or shared pool?
- [ ] **Capacity** — how much busy-season surge can be absorbed, and how fast?
- [ ] **Working hours overlap** — hours of coverage in US and AU time zones
- [ ] **Review layer** — who reviews before work returns to firm, and their credentials?

### Risk & Compliance (deal closers)

- [ ] **Confidentiality** — client-data handling under firm's obligations; whose engagement letter governs
- [ ] **IRC 7216 consent** — how third-party disclosure consent is handled for US tax work
- [ ] **Data residency** — where client data is stored and processed
- [ ] **SOC 2 evidence** — Type II report date, auditor, whether shareable under NDA (homepage claims this)
- [ ] **Professional indemnity insurance** — cover amount and limits
- [ ] **White-label mechanics** — does work carry firm's branding; do staff appear under firm name to clients?

### Proof Points (credibility)

- [ ] **2–3 firm outcomes** — firm size, work outsourced, measured result (anonymised OK, invented is not)
- [ ] **References** — are referenceable firms available for new prospects?

### Page Strategy

- [ ] **Target depth** — agree on word count based on competitor pages, measured during benchmark phase
