## Why this page first

`/partners/` targets the segment with the largest contract value — CPA firms

page on the site. It already owns the intent, so deepening concentrates signal
instead of splitting it.

## What a CPA firm principal needs answered

These are the decision questions that determine whether a firm engages an
outsourcing partner. Every one needs a real answer from the business.

**Commercial**
- `[NEEDED: pricing model]` — per hour, per client, per FTE, or retainer? Actual
  ranges, not "competitive pricing."
- `[NEEDED: minimum engagement]` — is there a floor, in hours or clients?
- `[NEEDED: contract terms]` — notice period, ramp-up and ramp-down terms.

**Delivery**
- `[NEEDED: onboarding timeline]` — the homepage FAQ says 2–3 weeks for direct
  clients. Is the CPA-firm path the same?
- `[NEEDED: staffing model]` — dedicated staff per firm, or a shared pool?
- `[NEEDED: capacity]` — how much busy-season surge can be absorbed, and how fast?
- `[NEEDED: working hours overlap]` — hours of overlap with US and AU time zones.
- `[NEEDED: review layer]` — who reviews before work returns to the firm, and
  what are their credentials?

**Risk and compliance** — the questions that actually block a deal
- `[NEEDED: confidentiality]` — client-data handling under the firm's own
  obligations; whose engagement letter governs.
- `[NEEDED: 7216 consent]` — how consent for disclosure to a third party is
  handled for US tax work.
- `[NEEDED: data residency]` — where client data is stored and processed.
- `[NEEDED: SOC 2 evidence]` — the homepage FAQ claims SOC 2 Type II. Report
  date, auditor, and whether it can be shared under NDA.
- `[NEEDED: insurance]` — professional indemnity cover and limits.
- `[NEEDED: white-label mechanics]` — does work carry the firm's branding; do
  staff appear under the firm's name to its clients?

**Proof**
- `[NEEDED: 2–3 firm outcomes]` — firm size, work outsourced, measured result.
  Anonymised is fine; invented is not.
- `[NEEDED: references]` — are referenceable firms available?

## Structure

1. H1 — states the white-label proposition for CPA firms in one line
2. Who this is for — firm profile, so the wrong reader self-selects out
3. How the engagement works — onboarding through delivery to review
4. What you can outsource — by function, with explicit exclusions
5. Commercials — the pricing model, stated plainly
6. Risk, compliance and confidentiality — the section that closes or loses deals
7. Proof — outcomes
8. FAQ — from the questions above
9. CTA to `/schedule/`
